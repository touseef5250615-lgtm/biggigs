Executive Summary

This analysis covers 50 sales transactions across 5 major cities and 5 product categories. Completed orders dominate the dataset, generating a total revenue of Rs 25,17,500 with an average transaction value of Rs 50,350.

Laptops account for the largest share of sales, and Lahore contributes the highest order count.

Key Insights

  • Top Customers: Ali Raza (8 orders), Sara Ahmed (7), Usman Tariq (7) drive a significant portion of sales.
  • Geographic Concentration: Lahore (18) and Karachi (14) together account for 64% of all transactions.
  • Product Mix: Laptops (15) lead the portfolio, followed by Mouse (11) and Keyboard, Monitor, Printer (8 each).
  • Order Value: Average unit price is Rs 37,590, with average quantity per order of 2.48.
  • Order Status: 41 Completed, 6 Pending, 3 Cancelled.

Recommendations

  • Customer Loyalty Program: Target top 5 customers with tiered incentives.
  • Geographic Expansion: Pilot targeted marketing in Islamabad and Faisalabad.
  • Cross-Sell Accessories: Bundle laptops with high-margin accessories.
  • Resolve Pending Orders: Implement rapid follow-up workflow to reduce 18% non-completion rate.
  • Dynamic Pricing: Evaluate price elasticity for laptops and accessories.

Conclusion

The sales data reveals a concentrated revenue base driven by a few high-value customers and cities, with laptops as the primary revenue generator. Focused loyalty incentives, geographic diversification, and strategic bundling are poised to elevate revenue growth.
